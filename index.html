About

My research asks how models can learn from different kinds of visual information—and still remain reliable when the data, domain, or task changes.

I am a Computer Science Ph.D. student at Wayne State University, advised by Prof. Dongxiao Zhu in the Trustworthy AI Lab. My work spans image segmentation, vision-language learning, foundation models, and AI evaluation.

I enjoy turning difficult research questions into reproducible systems: building data pipelines, designing controlled experiments, and translating results across disciplines.
